Common questions

How long do UPS batteries last?

Sealed lead-acid (VRLA) batteries typically last three to five years. Heat shortens their life considerably, so batteries in hot rooms without air-conditioning often need replacing sooner.

How do I know my batteries need replacing?

Shorter backup time than when the UPS was new, a failed battery self-test, a battery warning on the display, or batteries that look swollen or leak. A load test tells you for certain.

What happens to the old batteries?

Lead-acid batteries must be recycled through authorised channels under India's Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022. We collect your old batteries and send them for proper recycling.
