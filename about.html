Our story

We started by supplying computer peripherals to the Telugu film industry. Today, cinemas, hospitals, government bodies and pharma companies across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana rely on us to keep their power on.

Built on the film industry

Starlite Computer Services began as a small computer peripherals shop. Through a friend in Tollywood, our founder started supplying production houses with the equipment they needed, and the business grew one film and one studio at a time.

Working with production houses and cinemas showed us how much depends on uninterrupted power. A projector that cuts out mid-show or an edit suite that loses a day's work is a real cost. UPS systems became the heart of what we do.

Today we have completed more than 600 installations and supported over 600 theatres across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. We are an authorised partner of Delta Electronics, with offices in Hyderabad and Vijayawada.
