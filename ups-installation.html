Common questions

What does my site need before installation?

A ventilated space with room for the UPS and batteries, a correctly rated input supply and cabling, and proper earthing. Poor earthing is one of the most common causes of UPS faults, so we check it during the site survey.

Will installation disrupt my operations?

Small units can usually be installed without shutting anything down. For larger three-phase systems, we plan the changeover around a shutdown window that suits you, such as a night or weekend.

What do we get at handover?

A load-tested, commissioned UPS, a walkthrough for your team on alarms and basic operation, and a record of the settings and work done.
