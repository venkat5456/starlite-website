Common questions

How often should a UPS be serviced?

Most sites benefit from preventive visits every quarter or every six months. Critical sites and dusty or hot environments need more frequent checks. We set the visit frequency in your contract based on the load and site conditions.

What happens during a maintenance visit?

We clean and inspect the UPS, check connections, fans and capacitors, test the batteries, review alarm logs and load levels, and leave a written report of what we found.

Does a maintenance contract cover breakdowns?

Contract customers get priority response when a UPS fails. What is covered for parts and labour depends on the contract type you choose, and we explain the options clearly before you sign.
