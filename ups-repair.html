Common questions

What are the signs a UPS needs repair?

Repeated beeping or alarms, error codes on the display, much shorter backup time than before, overheating, unusual noise, or the UPS not switching to battery during a power cut.

Should I repair my UPS or replace it?

It depends on the age of the unit, the cost of the fault and whether spares are still available. After diagnosis we give you the repair cost and, if replacement makes more sense, tell you honestly.

Can my equipment keep running while the UPS is repaired?

Often yes. Many UPS systems have a bypass that feeds equipment directly from mains during repair, and for critical sites we can arrange a rental UPS in the meantime.
