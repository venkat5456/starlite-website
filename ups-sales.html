Common questions

What is the difference between online, line-interactive and offline UPS?

An online (double-conversion) UPS runs your equipment from its inverter all the time, so there is no switchover gap and the output is always clean. It suits servers, medical and lab equipment. A line-interactive UPS corrects voltage swings and switches to battery in a few milliseconds, which suits office networks. An offline UPS is the most basic type, for individual desktops.

What size UPS do I need?

Add up the power drawn by everything you want to protect and allow headroom for growth, usually 20 to 25 percent. UPS ratings are given in kVA and kW, and the two are not always equal, so it is worth having us do the calculation. We do a load assessment as part of every quote.

How long will the UPS run on battery?

Backup time depends on the battery bank and how heavily the UPS is loaded. The same UPS can run for minutes or hours depending on the batteries fitted. Tell us how long you need to run, for example until the generator starts or long enough to shut down safely, and we size the batteries to match.
