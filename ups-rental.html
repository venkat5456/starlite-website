Common questions

When does renting a UPS make sense?

For events, planned shutdowns, office moves, system migrations, or to cover a site while its own UPS is being repaired or replaced.

What do you need to know to quote a rental?

What equipment needs protecting, whether your supply is single-phase or three-phase, how long you need backup during an outage, the rental period and the site location.

Do you set it up?

Yes. We deliver the UPS, connect it safely, test it with your load and collect it at the end of the rental period.
