Which Delta family fits your load?

Pick roughly how much equipment you need to protect. We confirm the exact model after a load assessment.

Agilon family

Delta's Agilon family covers loads under 1 kVA, such as a single desktop, a router or a small CCTV recorder. For anything that can't afford to drop, we usually suggest stepping up to an online Amplon unit.

Amplon family

Single-phase online UPS for offices, billing counters, CCTV, network racks and individual medical or lab equipment. The INX and N series are the usual choice; R and RT series fit into server racks.

See the Amplon range

Amplon RT or Ultron

This is where the decision depends on your supply. With a single-phase supply, the Amplon N 10 kVA or RT 5 to 10 kVA fits. With three-phase, the Amplon RT 15/20 kVA or an entry Ultron HPH unit gives more room to grow.

Amplon RT Ultron HPH

Ultron family

Three-phase online UPS for whole floors, hospitals, pharma plants, multiplexes and server rooms. HPH series for IT and medical loads, NT series for factories and harsh environments.

See the Ultron range

Ultron DPS or Modulon

For large data centres and campuses, the Ultron DPS series runs units in parallel to reach multi-megawatt capacity. If you expect the load to keep growing, a Modulon modular system lets you add power modules instead of replacing the UPS.

Also available: Modulon modular UPS

Modulon systems, from 20 kVA, are built from hot-swappable power modules. You buy the capacity you need now and add modules as the load grows, and a failed module can be swapped without shutting the system down.
