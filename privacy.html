Privacy policy

How Starlite Computer Services handles the information you share with us through this website.

What we collect

When you send a request through our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, the service you are interested in and your message. If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we receive the details you share there.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your request, prepare quotes, and provide the services you ask for. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who else handles it

  • Messages from the contact form are delivered to our inbox by a form delivery service, Web3Forms.
  • Office maps on the contact page are provided by Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when the map loads.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Cookies and storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your request and any service that follows. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email us at srinivasrao@starlitecomputerservices.com.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page.
